## Interactive labs

All eight labs provide labeled controls, accessible diagram descriptions, readable fixed chart axes, a six-trial prediction notebook, evidence reflection and reset. Modes select appropriate starting views for relevant units. A subject lab is shared across its course, with lesson-specific challenges; this does not claim 74 separate simulation engines.

- Chemistry: amount/concentration, strong-acid titration, first-order decay, enzyme saturation and ideal-gas paths. Titration assumes a monoprotic strong acid/base at 25°C; water autoionization keeps equivalence finite. Enzyme and decay modes are ideal teaching models.
- Rocks: test six synthetic unknowns using hardness, streak, breakage and other observations; gather at least three before checking identification. Explore transitions in the rock cycle. Specimen geometry is schematic and not diagnostic.
- Circuits: series/parallel networks, common open/closed switch, resistor-limited LED and five Boolean gates with truth tables. Ideal DC networks conserve current and voltage. LED behavior uses a constant forward drop, with reverse current modeled as zero; it omits breakdown and device ratings.
- Water: conservative freshwater/seawater mixing, a separate oxygen input/demand budget and habitat comparisons. Oxygen clips at zero and shows when the linear model is depleted. Salinity and oxygen controls are deliberately independent.
- Protein: rotate a synthetic 24-residue chain, change compaction/contact cutoff, select residues and highlight chemistry classes. Geometric contacts are invariant under rotation and are not a folding energy or a genuine PDB structure. Historical H1N1 examples are identified as historical.
- Genetics: choose parental genotypes, inspect all Punnett cells, change dominance assumptions, and separately explore Hardy–Weinberg frequencies. Population allele frequency does not derive from the two selected parents.
- Hydrology: vary rainfall, watershed area, runoff fraction and duration; inspect a triangular hydrograph whose integral equals runoff volume. A separate Darcy view exposes hydraulic conductivity, area, head drop and flow length.
- Thermodynamics: compare isothermal, isobaric, isochoric and reversible adiabatic ideal monatomic-gas paths; inspect water heating, latent-heat fractions and mass scaling; explore Carnot efficiency and refrigerator/heat-pump limits. Work by the gas is positive, and Q=ΔU+W. Rounded heating constants assume approximately one atmosphere. Carnot results are reversible limits.

Pure calculations live in `src/lib/extended-lab-models.ts`; interactive presentation is in `src/components/extended-subject-labs.tsx`. Shared notebook/plot primitives are in `subject-lab-frame.tsx`. Gas-mode notebook records reflect the live nested controls.
