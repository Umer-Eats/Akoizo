## Subject alignment and source limitations

Subject rotation matching is not a claim that every historical question complies with every 2027 subtopic or level restriction. The expanded alignment/scoring note on each paper states the differences before the student starts. Original historical questions remain intact.

- Anatomy 2023 covers the 2027 respiratory/digestive/immune rotation. The more recent nervous/endocrine/sensory rotation was not substituted.
- Heredity papers cover the same genetics subject area but do not exhaust the expanded 2027 population-genetics syllabus. Some questions are now restricted to States/Nationals. BullSO also includes one gel-electrophoresis question beyond the 2027 techniques list.
- Disease Detectives retains the same three subject areas. BullSO's bias/confounding/herd-immunity items cover current States/Nationals material. MCQ 8 asks a specific disease-category association outside the current rules; the student note identifies that exception.
- Freshwater Dynamic Planet matches 2027; oceanography and glacier-rotation papers were not included. Recent everyday-weather/climate Meteorology and freshwater Water Quality papers were excluded because 2027 uses different topics.
- UT Austin follows its published key, including a disputed incomplete-dominance answer in question 25. Berkeley's key is headed BBSO but matches its paper; source terminology in questions 18 and 28 is retained. Columbia's written answers 11–13 are matched to the corresponding questions by content; key weights take precedence where they differ from paper weights, and written question 40 uses the paper's weight because its key does not state one. Multi-select question 24 requires both correct choices.
- National test packs offered for purchase were not acquired. No invented questions or competition provenance fill gaps in the library.

## Grading and persistence

Authenticated server grading ignores client scores. MCQ keys, numerical tolerances, accepted short-answer variants and rubric criteria stay in the server catalog; initial paper responses omit them. Submitted results include corrections: correct MCQ choices are green, written rubric answers are red, and exact student input remains visible.

MCQ sets require all correct choices. Numerical answers support decimal/scientific notation, fractions, stated units and published tolerance ranges. Equivalent notation not recognized by the parser goes to review. Recognized short answers grade automatically; unmatched nonempty prose remains pending, rather than receiving a fabricated semantic grade. Blank fields score zero. Instructors review each pending response against the linked full rubric and enter bounded partial credit. Scores and percentages are provisional until that review is complete. This is not fully automatic essay grading.

Submission UUIDs are scoped to the authenticated user and paper, making retries idempotent. A saved attempt completes at most one pending Practice assignment in that event, increases practice completion totals and awards no ranked points. Exact answers and reviewed scores are persisted in `practice_submissions`; `test_attempts` remains the dashboard record. Drafts are saved only in that student's browser under user/division/test-specific keys. Previous-attempt review preserves an unsubmitted draft.

## Extending and verifying

Edit `scripts/build-practice-catalog.mjs`, then run `node scripts/build-practice-catalog.mjs`. Verify the actual competition date, level, division, complete test, every answer-key marking, weights, all diagrams and 2027 subject rotation before adding a paper. Do not infer a year from an upload date. Explicitly record disagreements and practice-specific weighting. Keep test IDs stable because they identify saved submissions.

Run `npm test`, `npm run typecheck`, and `npm run build`. Catalog validation checks question IDs, correct choices, criterion totals and overall totals. Persistence tests cover cross-school review restrictions, forged score rejection, duplicate submissions, preserved text and assignment completion. `node --experimental-strip-types scripts/practice-browser-check.mjs` checks the real UI with isolated mocked accounts; it expects port 3002 unless `TEST_BASE_URL` is set. It creates no real accounts or student records. Screenshots are stored in ignored `documents/qa/`.
