# Akoizo male lab rat — concept v1

**Approved for implementation on October 6, 2026.** The owner requested a flat monochrome blue SVG and blue accents throughout the website. These original lavender concepts are retained as design history. The live icon files are `public/akoizo-logo.svg` and `public/favicon.svg`. The current articulated character uses `public/art/ako/parts.png` and `public/art/ako/rig.json`; the recolored still `public/art/ako-rat-blue.png` supplied its identity reference. The character's name remains **Ako**. See [design and motion](../../DESIGN.md) and [animation details](../../MASCOT_ANIMATION.md).

## Files

- `ako-male-rat-pixel.png`: generated transparent pixel-art mascot, full body, waving.
- `ako-rat-logo-concept.png`: earlier generated full-color logo exploration; the SVG direction has since been simplified at the owner's request.
- `ako-rat-logo.svg`: minimal, single-color editable rat mark. It retains the ears, forelock, goggles, eyes, and tapered muzzle without the colored illustration detail. This is vector geometry, not an embedded PNG.
- `ako-rat-favicon.svg`: simplified single-color icon with heavier lines and no whiskers for small sizes. Transparent background; not connected to app metadata.

The SVG is a simplified interpretation of the generated logo rather than an exact trace. Both archived SVGs use only one visible color (`#c6b3ff`) and a transparent background. Their live versions use blue (`#3b82f6`). The pixel mascot remains colorful. This folder's original mascot is one still pose. The website now animates separate illustrated parts around fixed joints, with procedural eyes and a curved tail; the earlier six-pose sheet is retired.

## Direction

A cute male ivory rat with a tousled forelock, plum eyes, lavender goggles, natural pink ears and tail, a friendly grin, and a small lab coat. The logo keeps the face and goggles while the pixel mascot carries the full character.

## Image generation

Both PNGs were created with the built-in imagegen tool, with transparent background enabled. The mascot revises the initial lab-rat concept; the logo uses that revised male mascot as an identity reference. No CLI image generation or raster editing was used.

### Mascot prompt

Edit the provided Akoizo pixel-art lab rat mascot into a clearly boyish MALE rat character. Keep the same original rat identity, ivory fur, natural pink inner ears and tail, lavender laboratory goggles on his forehead, small white lab coat with lavender trim, standing three-quarter pose with one paw waving, transparent background and retro 2D pixel-art rendering. Specific changes: remove the eyelash-like flicks and eyeliner accents from the eyes, remove rosy cheek makeup/blush, give him a small tousled ivory fur tuft between the goggles and ears, slightly broader cheerful muzzle, a playful confident grin and friendly curious expression. Keep him adorable, soft, approachable and youthful, not muscular or aggressive; no beard or human facial hair. Eyes should be simple round shiny dark-plum pixel eyes without long eyelashes. This is a cute male animal scientist mascot. Preserve the long rat tail, proportions, clothing and overall composition. Improve the pixel-art consistency with deliberate hard square pixels and a restrained palette; no smooth rendering or 3D. All ears, feet and tail remain within the image, isolated on genuinely transparent alpha. No text, scenery, sparkles, labels, frames or watermark. One character only.

### Logo prompt

Use case: logo-brand. Create a single original Akoizo logo icon based on the attached MALE lab-rat pixel mascot. Attached image is an identity reference, not an edit target. Translate his cute ivory rat head, pink inner ears, small tousled forelock, natural rose nose and lavender science goggles into a simple clean flat vector-style head logo. Front-facing symmetrical compact rat head, moderately rounded ears at upper sides, slightly tapering muzzle, goggles perched above the eyes with two broad round-cornered lenses and a bridge, small central pointed fur tuft, two friendly simple solid dark plum oval eyes without eyelashes, tiny rounded pink triangular nose, small curved confident smile, exactly two short whiskers each side. HEAD ONLY, no coat or body. Bold consistent deep plum outline, ivory face, blush inner ears and nose, lavender goggle frame and light lavender lenses. Minimal geometric shapes, extremely clean silhouette, suitable for a website header and future SVG reconstruction. No texture, no gradients, no shadows, no 3D, no pixel edges in this logo version, no tiny decorative marks. Genuine transparent alpha background with ample margins. One icon only, square composition, no text/wordmark/badge/watermark. Maintain the friendly male mascot identity from the reference.
