# More lesson diagrams

This second batch adds **33 distinct external diagrams to 25 lessons**, bringing the reference catalog from 57 to **90 figures**. The batch prioritizes lessons that previously had no external reference figure. Images were found and their captions inspected through Firecrawl.

Each placement has descriptive alternative text, an original reading prompt, visible author and license information, a source-page link, and the existing enlargement viewer. The original source images remain unmodified and load lazily. Teachers are not named in lesson content.

## Interpretation choices

- Photosynthesis diagrams separate electron flow, proton gradients, absorption spectra, and net photosynthetic rate.
- Stellar diagrams distinguish thermal spectra, shell burning, evolutionary tracks, and isochrones.
- The thermodynamic path diagram distinguishes path-dependent work from state-dependent internal energy.
- Water-quality diagrams distinguish standing biomass from energy flow and show persistent-pollutant biomagnification without generalizing to all contaminants.
- Blood-type diagrams are used for evidence interpretation and explicitly distinguish a compatible type from unique source identification.
- Genetic diagrams keep genotype ratios, phenotype ratios, small-sample test-cross uncertainty, pedigree constraints, and conditional X-linked probabilities separate.
- Circuit diagrams distinguish conductance from resistance slopes and avoid presenting the approximate silicon-diode voltage as a universal LED threshold.
