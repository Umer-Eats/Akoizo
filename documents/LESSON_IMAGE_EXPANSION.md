# Additional external lesson figures

Added 39 externally sourced images, diagrams, and charts to the existing 18 reference figures: **57 total**, covering all 16 supported Division C courses. Existing lesson prose, simulations, and assignments remain available.

Firecrawl was used to discover and read the source pages and their figure captions. Each new placement has a specific title, descriptive alternative text, an original interpretation prompt, a source link, author credit, and the applicable reuse terms. Figures remain unmodified on their original hosts and use the existing enlarge/zoom viewer. Source links remain available if a host becomes unavailable. Anatomy and Forensics now receive centrally catalogued figures as well as the courses built through the course builder.

## Scientific interpretation

The work-sign diagram explicitly translates chemistry's work-on convention to the thermodynamics lesson's work-by convention. The Vigenère square is labelled as a comparison rather than a Porta key. General electrophoresis photographs are distinguished from an STR case and its required labels and controls. The statistical charts prompt evaluation of association, outliers, and causal limits. Each figure appears beside the relevant section rather than being repeated throughout the course.
